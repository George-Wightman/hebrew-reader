Settings

Google's free tier needs no credit card and allows far more lookups per day than you'd ever use. Without a key the Sentence pad still works — it copies a question for you to paste into any AI chat instead. A backup key is only used after the first one's daily limit is fully spent — it isn't split between the two.

None of the actions on this page can delete a word from your library.

HousekeepingSafe. Nothing here loses anything.

Un-dismiss words

Dismissed suggestions get offered again next time they show up in a message.

Clear all marks

Unstars every word. The words themselves aren't touched.

Review prefixed words

Some words got filed with a ה (the) or ו (and) still attached — ha-izdamnut instead of izdamnut. This lists them and re-files each under the base word, carrying its drill history, marks, pairs and cached audio across. Fixed expressions like be-hatslakha and infinitives like lishloakh are left alone.

Clear form queue

A word whose forms failed to generate three times gets parked so the queue stops retrying it. This unparks everything and resets today's call count. Generated forms are kept.

Mark Duolingo batch as known

Fast-forwards the spaced-repetition schedule for the words added from Duolingo — for the ones you can already say on sight, so sessions and conversations treat them as trained instead of brand new. Skips any that are already strong, so it never undoes real practice.

Fine-tune word strength

Every word in your Library, with where the app thinks you are on understanding it and on speaking it. Click to set either one directly — for anything the schedule has wrong, not just the Duolingo batch.

Retire words you already know

Moves every word that's strong on both understanding and speaking off the focus grid into reserve. They stay in your library — still drilled, searched and exported — just not filling the wall. Starred words are skipped.

Retire untouched imports

The Duolingo and starter batches added words you never picked yourself. This retires the ones that have never appeared in a real message, clearing them off the focus grid in one go. Starred words are skipped, and any of them can be brought back with ↑ or by starring it.

Clean up nonsense sentences

Re-checks every banked sentence and removes only the ones that don't actually make sense. Everything that passes keeps its history.

ResetsClears something specific. Your library stays intact.

Reset "words I know"

Forgets words you've marked as already-known and spelling variants the pad has learned. They'll be flagged again.

Forget corrected spellings

A separate list from "words I know" above — this is text you've personally corrected while reading a message. Clearing it means those corrections stop applying.

Bin the practice sentences

Deletes every banked practice sentence outright, unread. Your library, word strengths and progress all stay exactly as they are — new sentences get written next session.

Reset drill schedule

This is the one that resets progress, not your words: every word becomes due for review again, as if freshly added. Nothing is deleted — your library, marks and banked sentences are untouched.

Overwrites custom dataThe one place a mis-click can lose something you made yourself.

Fix opposites

Resets every opposite pairing in your library to the curated built-in set — silently discarding any pairing you've dragged together yourself that isn't in it.

Word strength

Click a dot to set that word's strength directly — for understanding it (hearing it) and for speaking it, scored separately because they're genuinely different skills here. This is what the session builder and Conversation mode actually check before they'll use a word, so fixing it here changes what you get asked next time.
